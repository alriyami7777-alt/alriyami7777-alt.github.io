04 / RESEARCH APPROACH

Ask what the
evidence supports.

01

Respect the timeline

Keep future information out of earlier predictions and examine how splitting and preprocessing affect evaluation.

02

Measure rare outcomes

Look beyond accuracy. Study precision–recall behaviour and the consequences of threshold choices.

03

Test the limits

Distinguish benchmark findings from operational claims, and test explanation faithfulness rather than assume it.
