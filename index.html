DATASET FIELD GUIDE

Understand the evidence behind the model.

Three public datasets relevant to my cross-dataset research. Each captures a different setting; their origins and labels matter when interpreting results.

SYNTHETIC ACTIVITY

CERT

Carnegie Mellon University · Software Engineering Institute

A collection of generated background activity and malicious-user scenarios for insider-threat research. Releases include documentation and an answer key describing the simulated attacks.

Research context Useful for controlled benchmark experiments; synthetic behaviour does not establish performance in an operational environment.

Explore CERT and downloads

EXERCISE + SIMULATED + SYNTHETIC

SPEDIA

Universidad Politécnica de Madrid research team

Combines user activity from a controlled cyber exercise, simulated role-based behaviour, and synthetic events derived from CERT. Event logs support the study of insider-threat detection.

Research context Check which components are used before treating a transfer test as independent of CERT.

Explore SPEDIA and downloads

NETWORK LOGS + RED-TEAM EVENTS

LANL

Los Alamos National Laboratory

The Comprehensive, Multi-Source Cyber-Security Events dataset covers 58 days of de-identified enterprise activity, including authentication, processes, DNS and network flows, alongside identified red-team events.

Research context Useful for temporal and network analysis. Red-team activity represents a specific threat scenario, so label definitions need care.

Explore LANL and downloads

The linked source pages provide documentation, citation details and access information. Dataset ownership remains with the credited creators. Read the note on dataset provenance.

07 / RESEARCH APPROACH

Ask what the
evidence supports.

01

Respect the timeline

Keep future information out of earlier predictions and examine how splitting and preprocessing affect evaluation.

02

Measure rare outcomes

Look beyond accuracy. Study precision–recall behaviour and the consequences of threshold choices.

03

Test the limits

Distinguish benchmark findings from operational claims, and test explanation faithfulness rather than assume it.
